“To those on Earth, Space, and all people who live in this world.”

“I am Mineva Lao Zabi.”

“For the rudeness of addressing you all in this manner, please forgive me.”

“I am the heir to the Zabi family, who once led the Principality of Zeon.”

“However, what I am about to tell you has nothing to do with my origins.”

“Today, I learned a secret that relates to the very foundation of the Federal Government.”

“As just one human living in this world, I would like to share that secret with you all.”

“The beginning of the Universal Century marked the creation of the Universal Century Charter.”

“You may all be familiar with the monument that is the foundation of the Federation Government and the cornerstone of its policy-making.”

“It was built 96 years ago in the Prime Minister's official residence, Laplace, and was believed to have been lost in a terrorist bombing that occurred shortly after.” “However, it was not lost.”

“While a replica monument is displayed in the parliament building in Dakar, the original monument has been hidden for a long time.”

“Please take a look for yourself.”

“Right now, what you see behind me now is the real Universal Century Charter created at Laplace.”

“I belive you can see it.”

“The real Universal Century Charter had an additional chapter that we don't know about.”

“‘If in the future it is recognized that a new human race adapted to space emerges, they will be given priority in participating in government administration…’”

“That was the clause written in the chapter titled ‘The Future.’”

“Of course, this was not a reference to Newtypes.”

“‘Humanity that advances into space will expand its cognitive capabilities and be able to understand each other without misunderstanding…’”

“Zeon Deikun advocated his theory of Newtypes more than 40 years after this charter was created.”

“The Laplace Incident buried a passage which should have been nothing more than a prayer for the distant future.”

“But that coincidence turned the charter into a curse.”

“What if this passage was linked to Zeon's theory of Newtypes and sparked the Spacenoid independence movement?”

“At first, the monument merely told the truth about the Laplace Incident, but from then on it became a source of fear that could overthrow the Federal Government.”

“Fearing its existence, the Federation conspired with the Vist Foundation, which had obtained the monument, to seal it away, calling it ‘Laplace’s Box.’”

“It may have been an act of malice, but it was also an unavoidable decision to maintain peace.”

“However, as this system was passed down, the original meaning of the ‘Box’ was lost, and a mentality that shunned anything Newtype-like took root.”

“After the nightmare of the One Year War, that mentality became the very essence of the rigidity of the Federation Government.”

“In a few more years, the nation that bears the name of Zeon will disappear, and the Newtype myth will be buried in the darkness of history.”

“That is fine.”

“I have no intention of using the publication of this fact to denounce the Federation or to link it to the revival of Zeon.”

“If anyone intends to do so, I shall declare, as Mineva Zabi, that I will correct that thinking.”

“Laplace’s Box was never meant for this.”

“Laplace's Box was a box that contained the kindness of people.”

“A hundred years ago, we spacenoids were sent into space with that same kindness.”

“Even if it was a deception to soothe our consciences, or a prayer with results we could not take responsibility for, please think about the hearts of those who had no choice.”

“They chose what they thought was the best option from a limited number of options, and entrusted their hopes to future generations.”

“While our issues differ in magnitude, we do encounter the same struggles as they do.”

“Shall we keep the Box? Shall we seal it? Shall we open it completely?”

“Everything was done for humanity.”

“It is our sin as humans, our just rewards, it can become a possibility of destruction, but it can also become hope.”

“People's desire to live better and avoid injustice has moved this world forward, even if only little by little.”

“When the Earth could no longer support the advanced civilization that had developed in this way, our ancestors sent the overpopulated human population into space.”

“Although this act inevitably drew criticism as an abandonment of the people, its roots lie in the goodwill of wanting to save people and the Earth.”

“In the future, if the emergence of a new human race adapted to space is recognized, these people will be given priority in participating in government administration.”

“This passage, added to the end of the Universal Century Charter on the last night of Anno Domini, can also be said to have been woven from limitless goodwill.”

“It all started with good intentions, and whether or not we can turn this into good intentions depends entirely on our attitude.”

“If we change, the world will change.”

“Even if we don't become Newtypes, each of us has a heart that feels, and the power to change according to our environment.”

“Nothing will begin if we deny the karma of humans and seek salvation in the horizons of Newtypes.”

“No matter how inconvenient or frustrating it may be, all we can do is continue the good intentions woven through our blood.”

”After all, everything we need should be contained within the body.”

“Ricardo Marcenas once said, ‘There is no need to be troubled about other people writing misleading scripts in your life. Just use the God in you to look clearly at the future that is about to begin.’”

“Nearly a hundred years have passed since then, and we are standing at the starting point again.”

“My words are my own, and this is what I truly believe."

“To all of you listening to this broadcast, please see the truth with your own eyes.”

“And, just as people did a hundred years ago, I would like you to think about the next hundred years with good intentions.”

“So may what sleeps in us all, our dormant possibility, our god within, be believed…”
